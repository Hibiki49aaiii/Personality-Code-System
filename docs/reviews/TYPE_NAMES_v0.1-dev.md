# C01D Draft Type Name Review Ledger — v0.1-dev

Status: **machine-valid / owner editorial approval pending**

This ledger exists so deterministic generation is not confused with editorial approval. Every reachable C01D code is listed side-by-side. `pending-owner` means the name is technically valid and traceable but has not been approved as a final public-facing name.

## Review vocabulary

- `pending-owner` — machine-valid, not public-name approved.
- `approve-draft` — acceptable for development-facing use; still not a Phase 5C public taxonomy approval.
- `revise` — wording should change without changing the Core Code semantics.
- `reject` — unsuitable wording; replacement required.

Automated/editorial risk flags:

- `review-credential-sounding` — may read like a professional qualification rather than a personality label.
- `review-length` — may be long in mobile/share-card typography.
- `review-mechanical` — semantically clear but may sound engineered rather than memorable.
- `review-neologism-relation` — `広縁/深縁` are deliberate coined labels and need native-language/connotation review.

## Cross-cutting adversarial observations

1. **`監査士`** is the highest-risk role label. It is compact and maps well to `L+V+G`, but `士` can sound like a credential. Alternatives should be evaluated before public approval.
2. **`独立検証者`** is accurate but long and technical. It may work in the dossier while being weak as an SNS identity label.
3. **`検証設計家` / `探究設計家`** preserve provenance very well but may feel mechanically constructed. They are acceptable engineering labels; memorability still needs human review.
4. **`広縁 / 深縁`** cleanly distinguish relationship-investment breadth/depth without falsely claiming extroversion/introversion, but they are coined terms. Their readability and unintended meanings require explicit approval.
5. **`深化 / 構想 / 実践 / 開拓`** are currently the strongest part of the naming grammar: compact, differentiated, and directly tied to EXE/NOV poles. They should still be tested as a family, not independently.
6. The `{action}の{role}〈relationship〉` grammar is intentionally regular. That consistency improves explainability but can reduce the collectible/character-like feel. Public launch may retain the regular structural title while adding a separate branded nickname only if that nickname has a strict editorial contract and does not add psychological claims.
